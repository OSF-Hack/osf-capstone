# Demo script

Target duration: 2 minutes 30 seconds

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem

“An applicant completes a Nigerian driver's licence application, but the
process goes quiet. They may not know what stage failed, what the official
tracker needs, or where to escalate. Repeated visits cost time and money, and
the applicant still lacks a clear next step.”

Show the CivicRoute NG landing screen and the three-step explanation.

## 0:20–1:10 — The golden journey

“CivicRoute NG starts with the applicant's situation. It does not ask for a
name, licence number, application ID or date of birth.”

Select `FRSC biometric capture`, then select `The application appears stalled`.

“The guidance changes to the applicant's last confirmed stage. It explains
what to record, what to prepare and which official route to use. The applicant
can open the official tracker directly, then copy or print a private action
card.”

Open the tracker in a new tab, return, and use `Copy action card`.

## 1:10–1:40 — Escalation and discrepancies

“If tracking does not resolve the problem, CivicRoute NG narrows the official
support options instead of presenting another generic link directory. When
published guidance differs from reality, the applicant can create a
category-based discrepancy note. The pilot stores nothing and warns against
including sensitive information.”

Show the verified channels and copy one discrepancy note.

## 1:40–2:05 — Trust and inclusion

“Every material instruction remains traceable to an official source and a
human review date. Missing evidence stays visible. The guide does not invent a
response-time guarantee, diagnose biometric failures or claim access to FRSC
records. After the first visit, previously loaded guidance remains available
on a poor connection.”

Briefly show the evidence register and offline indicator.

## 2:05–2:30 — Scale and close

“The first service pack focuses on driver's licence tracking and escalation.
The same trust model can support other public services through reviewed service
packs, without building a parallel government database. CivicRoute NG turns
fragmented information into one safe, verified next action.”

End on the project name, `civicrouteng.vercel.app`, and the repository link.

## Recording checklist

- Use a 390 × 844 mobile viewport or a real phone.
- Hide bookmarks, notifications and personal accounts.
- Keep the pointer still while speaking.
- Do not enter real applicant details on the official tracker.
- Record one uninterrupted journey and export at 1080p.
- Keep the final file comfortably below the 250 MB upload limit.
