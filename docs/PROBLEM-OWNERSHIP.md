### Participant-provided household account

Ahmed and his father renewed their licences at approximately the same time
after a new capture technology had been introduced. Ahmed's application
progressed, but his father's did not. Staff reportedly attributed the delay to
his father's earlier biometric capture having been made while he wore a Hausa
cap and said the new technology was not working for those records. Ahmed
personally joined repeated visits to the FRSC office to ask whether the licence
was ready. The renewed licence took almost one year to arrive, during which the
previous licence had expired.

This account was supplied directly by the participant on 20 September 2026.
The staff explanation is recorded as remembered, not as a verified technical
cause. No conclusion is made that caps generally cause capture failures.
