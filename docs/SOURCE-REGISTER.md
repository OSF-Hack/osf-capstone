# Authoritative source register

Reviewed on 16 September 2026. Only official FRSC and Nigeria Driver's Licence platform pages are used for material guidance in the first service pack.

| Source | Supported claims |
|---|---|
| [Nigeria Driver's Licence Portal](https://nigeriadriverslicence.frsc.gov.ng/) | Application process stages; renewal/reissue overview; platform contact numbers |
| [Application Status Tracker](https://pay.nigeriadriverslicence.org/license/trackStatus) | Status lookup accepts Application ID or licence number, date of birth, and CAPTCHA |
| [Driver's Licence Support](https://nigeriadriverslicence.frsc.gov.ng/support) | Support form fields, platform email and support phone |
| [FRSC Contact and Feedback](https://frsc.gov.ng/contact-us/) | National enquiries and complaints channels |
| [FRSC Driver's Licence Centres](https://frsc.gov.ng/commands/driver-license-centers/) | State-by-state centre directory and published centre contacts |

## Known evidence gap

The reviewed official sources do not publish a guaranteed support response time or a complete public definition for every possible application-status value. The proof of concept must not invent either.
