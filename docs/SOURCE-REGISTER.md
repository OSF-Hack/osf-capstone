## Known evidence gap

The reviewed official sources do not publish a guaranteed support response time or a complete public definition for every possible application-status value. The proof of concept must not invent either.

The participant's household account includes a staff-attributed failure involving
a previous capture made while wearing a Hausa cap. No reviewed source verifies
headwear as a general cause of biometric failure. Treat this as a capture or
identity exception to validate, not as a technical fact.
