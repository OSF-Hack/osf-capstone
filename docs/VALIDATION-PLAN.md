# Stage 6: three short applicant validations

Status: current

Working evidence document: [OSF x Andela Hackathon — Validation & Submission Workspace](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1jc9FgLYW8FTVP3DUiQlzi68RdwTwlACxYzQpEYy8kvs)

## Goal

Test whether the tracking and escalation failure is repeated across real
applicants and whether the current golden journey helps without collecting
sensitive identifiers.

## Desk-research boundary

The participant's household account and published applicant cases strengthen
the problem evidence, but they do not replace the three sessions below. The
participant's father may be recorded as P1 only after fresh consent, the
interview questions and the prototype task are completed. Do not copy online
comments into P1–P3 records.

## Recruit three contrasting applicants

1. One person currently waiting for a licence or renewal.
2. One person who recently completed the process.
3. One person whose process stalled or required repeated centre visits.

Keep each interview to 5–8 minutes. Ask about the real experience before
showing the prototype. Record participants as P1, P2, and P3; do not record
application IDs, licence numbers, dates of birth, or identifying details.

## Recruitment message

Use this message through WhatsApp, SMS, or a direct call:

> Hello. I am testing a small prototype that helps Nigerian driver's licence
> applicants understand tracking and escalation options. I am looking for
> someone who has applied for or renewed a licence. The conversation takes
> about 5–8 minutes and is not connected to FRSC. I will not ask for your name,
> application ID, licence number, date of birth, payment details, or other
> sensitive information. Would you be willing to test it and share anonymous
> feedback?

Do not recruit an applicant by promising case resolution, faster processing,
or access to FRSC records.

## Session preparation

- Use the current application locally on a phone-sized viewport or let the
  participant use the facilitator's phone or laptop.
- Open the application before the session and confirm that the official links
  work.
- Open the working evidence document to the correct participant record.
- Assign only the anonymous code P1, P2, or P3.
- If the session is remote, share the screen and ask the participant to direct
  each action. Do not request a participant screen recording.

## Opening and consent

Read this before taking notes:

> We are testing a prototype, not you. It provides guidance and links to
> official services, but it cannot access or change an FRSC application. Please
> do not share an application ID, licence number, date of birth, payment data,
> or any identifying information. I would like to take anonymous notes. You may
> skip any question or stop at any time. May I continue and take anonymous
> notes?

Stop the session if the participant does not consent. Record only `No consent —
session stopped`; do not record their explanation.

## Interview before showing the prototype

Ask in this order without suggesting an answer:

1. Tell me about the last time you applied for or renewed a Nigerian driver's
   licence.
2. At what point did you stop knowing what was happening or what to do next?
3. How did you try to track the application, and what response did you get?
4. When tracking did not help, whom did you contact or where did you go?
5. What did the uncertainty cost you in time, transport, money, missed work,
   stress, or another consequence?
6. What information would have made the next step clear?
7. Which escalation channel would you trust, and what proof would you expect?

Do not ask whether the participant likes the proposed solution or whether an
offline tracker would solve the problem. Those questions lead the participant.

## Prototype task

Give the participant this scenario without explaining the interface:

> Imagine you submitted a driver's licence application and no longer know what
> stage it has reached. Use this prototype to work out what you would do next,
> how you would track it, and where you would escalate if tracking did not help.

Observe rather than teach. Record:

- the first control selected;
- whether the participant reaches the official tracker;
- whether they understand what the tracker requires;
- whether they find an appropriate escalation route;
- whether they find and understand the action card;
- the number of facilitator prompts; and
- the most serious hesitation or misunderstanding.

After the task, ask:

1. What was clear?
2. What was missing or confusing?
3. What would stop you from trusting or using this?
4. Would you save, print, or share the action card? Why?
5. What is the one change you would make first?

## Evidence gate

Proceed to Stage 7 only when:

- at least two of three applicants independently confirm the tracking or
  escalation problem; and
- all three can complete the golden journey without help or with no more than
  one minor prompt.

If the gate fails, revise the journey and retest before adding discrepancy
reporting.

Record the gate result as one of:

- `PASS — proceed to Stage 7`
- `REVISE — update the golden journey and retest`
- `INVALID — recruitment or consent requirements were not met`

## Output

- Three anonymised interview records
- A pattern synthesis
- A keep/change/do-not-build decision list
- At most one permission-cleared quotation per participant
