# Stage 6: three short applicant validations

Status: current

Working evidence document: [OSF x Andela Hackathon — Validation & Submission Workspace](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1jc9FgLYW8FTVP3DUiQlzi68RdwTwlACxYzQpEYy8kvs)

## Goal

Test whether the tracking and escalation failure is repeated across real
applicants and whether the current golden journey helps without collecting
sensitive identifiers.

## Recruit three contrasting applicants

1. One person currently waiting for a licence or renewal.
2. One person who recently completed the process.
3. One person whose process stalled or required repeated centre visits.

Keep each interview to 5–8 minutes. Ask about the real experience before
showing the prototype. Record participants as P1, P2, and P3; do not record
application IDs, licence numbers, dates of birth, or identifying details.

## Evidence gate

Proceed to Stage 7 only when:

- at least two of three applicants independently confirm the tracking or
  escalation problem; and
- all three can complete the golden journey without help or with no more than
  one minor prompt.

If the gate fails, revise the journey and retest before adding discrepancy
reporting.

## Output

- Three anonymised interview records
- A pattern synthesis
- A keep/change/do-not-build decision list
- At most one permission-cleared quotation per participant
